One way Anova test was used in order to find the most important features in every dataset.
Test results are described by F-statistic and p-value. The higher the F-statistic, the more important the feature is. The lower the p-value, the more significant the feature is.
Order of the features is based on the F-statistic value. The features with the highest F-statistic are considered the most important.
